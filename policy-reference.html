  • Antique+4–10% for consecutive years reporting (2y→4% … 5y+→10%).
  • Fix bypass+10% for bypassing a previously fixed bug. Stacks on top.

Tier 3 — leaks & misconfigurations

Non-applicative findings fixed by a config or access change, not a code patch. Not scored with CVSS — flat bounty by severity, one per source location.

  • qualifiesLeaked credentials or tokens in public repos, exposed internal docs, misconfigured buckets or dashboards with PII — demonstrably owned by Mercado Libre (corporate email in commits, internal references, in-scope host).
  • confirm onlyDo not access, exfiltrate, or reuse the data. A single non-destructive validation call is the limit — going further voids eligibility.
  • report withSource location (URL/path), ownership evidence, and whether the secret was active at discovery.
  • excludedReports from integrator (third-party developer) users are not accepted.

Tier 3 pays $1,000 / $750 / $200 / $75 (critical / high / medium / low). The Antique bonus applies; Newbie and Fix Bypass do not.

Key rules

Prohibited actions can get you banned from the program. Read the full policy before testing.

  • no scanNo massive automated scanning — creative manual testing instead.
  • no bruteNo brute force or rate-limit probing.
  • no DoSNo denial of service or service degradation.
  • no SENo social engineering, phishing, or spam.
  • no ATONever touch another user's account or data.
  • test usersUse provided or self-created test users; findings are scored Privileges Required: High.
  • XSSWithout a CSP bypass, rated lower or not at all.
  • mobileCert pinning, obfuscation, root/emulator detection, and in-app-browser deeplinks are out.
  • 3rd-partyMust be exploitable on ML infra; CVEs only 30 days after publication, with a PoC.
  • takeoverMust insert content; include DNS/provider evidence; no cookie theft or user interaction.
